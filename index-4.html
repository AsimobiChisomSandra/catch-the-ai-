    What this checker cannot do

    Flagging is not verifying. A highlighted part is a place to look, not a proven mistake. A part with no highlight is not proven true. It does not know if an answer is true. It only spots the details where AI often slips: numbers, dates, quotes, sources and big claims. A wrong answer with none of those can still look green. Green means "low-risk type, skim it". It never means "correct". The checking is still yours, just shorter.
